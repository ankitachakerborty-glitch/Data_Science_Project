Key insights (all segments)

  • Government drives profit: 67% of total profit from 44% of sales (margin 21.7%).
  • Enterprise loses money: $19.6M sales but a $-615K loss (-3.1% margin) — review pricing and cost.
  • Discounts erode margin: margin falls from 17.9% (Low band) to 9.1% (High band), while High-band deals add the most units.
  • Growth: Sep–Dec 2014 sales were 37% above Sep–Dec 2013 (the only overlapping months).
  • Products: Paseo leads profit; Carretera trails. Country profit is fairly even, led by France.
